This is the first stage (Stage one) of a two-stage competition: the first stage will evaluate the qualifications of

competition to proceed with Stage Two. Bidders that have qualified in Stage One /A will not be required to

and will be considered in Stage One /B.

sample is only being provided for bidders to reference the General Conditions.

APPENDIX 1 - STAGE ONE QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS 
 
DESCRIPTION: 
 
5P468-26-0011/A     Stage 1 - Johnston Canyon Rock Slope Remediation, Banff National Park 
 
Parks Canada Agency requires the services of a qualified contractor to complete rock slope remediation work 
along the Johnston Canyon Trail in Banff National Park. The work is part of Parks Canada’s ongoing rockfall hazard 
management that is intended to reduce the potential safety hazards along the busy trail which runs through the 
narrow, vertical walled canyon. The work will be situated in a remote environment with significant access and 
logistical constraints. 
 
The scope of work includes but is not limited to, rock scaling, trimming, rock bolt and rock dowel installations. 
 
Access to the site is restricted to pedestrian and small ATV means, with limited vehicular access and very limited 
on-site staging or laydown areas. All construction activities, materials, and equipment must be transported to and 
from the site using existing trail networks and/or helicopter-supported access, requiring careful planning and 
coordination to safely and efficiently execute the work within a sensitive national park environment.

Mandatory Requirements  
M1 
Rock Slope Remediation in Remote or Back-Country Environments 
 
The Bidder must provide two (2) reference projects that have been substantially completed within the last 
ten (10) years, where the projects were rock slope remediation projects that included rock scaling, rock 
bolt installation and disposal of material, and where work was performed in a remote or back-country 
environment with limited or no vehicular access, requiring helicopter support and off-site staging was 
utilized. 
 
The reference projects must demonstrate the Bidder’s ability to plan and execute rock slope remediation 
work under comparable constrained access conditions with helicopter-supported access. 
 
Only the two (2) reference projects, in sequence, will receive consideration and any other provided projects 
will not receive consideration. 
 
The projects preferably but need not necessarily have been in the public sector (i.e. work completed for a 
federal, provincial/territorial or municipal government client). 
 
For at least one (1) of the project examples, the Bidder must have been the Prime Contractor and not have 
been retained to complete the work under sub-contract. 
 
For each project, the component of the work completed by the Bidder related to rock scaling, rock anchor 
installation and disposal of material must have a value of at least $300,000 (excluding taxes).  
 
For each project, the bidder must provide a client reference (name of client, name of client representative 
and their position, current contact information) for each project. PCA reserves the right to verify 
information for completeness and accuracy and to verify the information presented only.
